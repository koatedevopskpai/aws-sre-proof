# Billing check, 20 November 2026 (partial reconciliation)

Earlier evidence in this repository records billing as unreconciled at the time it was captured. Those files are left unchanged.
This file records a later check.

## What was queried

AWS Cost Explorer, `us-east-1`, read-only, two API calls (Cost Explorer charges about USD 0.01 per request):

- `GetCostAndUsage`, `2026-10-29` to `2026-11-03` (end exclusive), `DAILY`, metric `UnblendedCost`, grouped by `SERVICE`
- the same query grouped by `RECORD_TYPE` (usage versus credit)

The query covers the whole account, not only this lab. No account identifier is recorded here.

- On the final-run day, account usage was **USD 5.44**, fully offset by credits, so the **net charge was USD 0**.
- The other days show a steady **about USD 0.14 per day** of usage that is not this lab (retained backend and unrelated account resources). Subtracting that gives a lab increment of **about USD 5.30** for 31 October, inside the planned USD 4-8 range and the USD 15 allowance.
- By service, the only line above USD 0.005 over the whole window was `EC2 - Other` (USD 0.53 positive in the by-service view; the by-record-type view shows the credit offset).

## What this does and does not establish

- It is a **partial reconciliation**: the final-run day only.
- Spend is **not attributed by tag**: no cost-allocation tags were active, so Cost Explorer could not filter by this lab's tag. The USD 5.30 figure is an inference (day total minus the neighbouring-days baseline), not a tagged measurement.
- **Earlier lab sessions** (including the earlier worker-replacement run) fall before 29 October and are **not covered**.
- 1-2 November were still flagged as estimated; the billing period was open.
- Credits mean the **cash cost was USD 0** even though usage was not. Usage, not cash, is the number to compare with the estimate.
- It is not an invoice.
